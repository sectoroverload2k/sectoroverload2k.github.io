## Overview

Triton Processing provides card processing for businesses, with a focus on dental and medical practices in North and South Carolina. Practices send one month's merchant statement and get every fee explained line by line — free, back in one business day — next to Triton's interchange-plus price.

## The Problem

Tiered pricing sorts transactions into qualified, mid-qualified and non-qualified buckets. Premium rewards and corporate cards — common for high-ticket elective care — often land in the most expensive bucket, and statements rarely explain why.

## How It Works

1. **Send a statement** — a PDF or phone photo of one month; no account credentials or patient information needed
2. **Line-by-line breakdown** — every fee identified and grouped, including ones with no plain-English label
3. **Side-by-side pricing** — interchange-plus pricing shown beside the current costs; if the current deal is fair, they say so

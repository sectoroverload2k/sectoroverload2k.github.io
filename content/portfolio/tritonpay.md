## Overview

TritonPay is a payment processing service from Triton Processing built around transparent interchange-plus pricing: businesses pay the actual wholesale interchange rate plus a small flat fee. It is currently taking waitlist sign-ups ahead of launch.

## Key Features

- **Interchange-plus pricing** — no markups or percentage padding
- **No hidden fees** — no statement, annual, PCI compliance or batch fees
- **No long-term contracts** — month-to-month, no termination fees
- **Next-business-day funding**
- **Merchant dashboard** — daily volume, transactions, card mix, entry method and a per-card cost-transparency breakdown

## Overview

Triton delivers the critical technology businesses rely on — payments, email, hosting and IT — from one accountable team, with its own AI agents doing much of the heavy lifting.

## Service Lines

- Card processing (through Triton Processing)
- Email hosting with DKIM, DMARC and bounce handling done right
- Web hosting and virtual servers
- IT consulting — architecture, scoping and roadmaps
- AI development (through Triton Agency)
- Phone services (business VoIP)
- Network cabling
- IT projects and migrations — greenfield builds, redesigns and datacenter-to-cloud migrations

## Product Family

Triton Processing (payments), Triton Email (sending backbone), On Triton (restaurant loyalty & signage, in build), Triton Agency (custom software & AI) and CathyBot (AI chat widgets).

## Approach

- Budgeted, scoped projects with a number and an end date
- Free merchant statement audits, including per-location audits for franchises and multi-site operators

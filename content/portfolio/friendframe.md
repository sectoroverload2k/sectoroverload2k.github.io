## Overview

FriendFrame puts the people you love on every TV. Family members send photos from their phones in a couple of taps, and the photos appear on a loved one's TV — keeping families close no matter the distance.

## Key Features

- **Share from anywhere** — send photos from a phone in a couple of taps
- **Big-screen display** — photos appear on the recipient's TV
- **Private by design** — only invited family can send photos
- **Roku** — runs on Roku TVs and streaming devices (channel coming soon to the Roku Channel Store); pair with an on-screen code
- Free to start, no card needed

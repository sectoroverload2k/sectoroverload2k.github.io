## Overview

Jack's Tube Trivia turns any room into a game show. The TV asks the questions, everyone's phone is the buzzer, and a live scoreboard tracks every answer. No app to download — players join from any phone browser. A Wazoo Games production.

## How It Works

1. **Launch on your TV** — open Jack's Tube Trivia on Roku; it shows the questions, music and live scoreboard
2. **Join on your phone** — open jacks.tube and type the room code; no install, no account
3. **Buzz in & score** — answer fast and watch the leaderboard update live

## Key Features

- 2–100 players, each on their own phone
- No downloads or logins
- Real-time scoreboard on the big screen

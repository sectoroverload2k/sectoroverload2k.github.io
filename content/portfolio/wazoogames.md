## Overview

Wazoo Games makes party games you play right on your Roku TV. The TV hosts the game and everyone's phone is the controller — no PC, no Steam, and no downloads for players.

## How It Works

1. **Launch on Roku** — open any Wazoo game and start a room; the TV becomes the stage
2. **Join on phones** — players open the room link and enter the on-screen code, no install
3. **Play together** — answer, draw, bluff and compete on the big screen

## Games

- **Jack's Tube Trivia** — trivia game show with phone buzzers (2–8 players)
- **Sketch Clash** — draw fast, guess faster (3–12 players)
- **Beat Battle** — name that tune (2–8 players)
- **Bluff Box**, **Word Rush**, **Meme Machine**
- Coming soon: **Clue Crew** and **Top Shelf**

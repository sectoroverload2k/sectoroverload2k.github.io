## Overview

SignaCast is digital signage software that turns any TV into a screen you control. Businesses design menus, boards and promotions in a drag-and-drop editor, put them on playlists and schedules, and play them on hardware they already own — smart TVs, streaming sticks, Raspberry Pi or kiosk PCs. No proprietary player box required.

## Key Features

### Designer
- Drag-and-drop editor built for TV and kiosk sizes (Full HD, 4K, portrait, custom)
- Templates for menus, posters, flyers and opening hours
- Brand kit with colors, logo and fonts; layers, snapping and guides
- Autosave, undo/redo and a design library

### Data-Driven Templates
- Upload a CSV, bind fields to text boxes and let a repeater lay out rows
- Automatic pagination, rule-based formatting, and re-rendering when the data changes
- Versioned uploads; push new data over the API from a POS or script

### Screens & Playlists
- Playlists with per-slide durations and crossfades
- Day/time schedules and multi-region screen layouts

### Player
- Player-agnostic: every screen gets a link that plays on existing hardware
- Keeps playing when the network drops
